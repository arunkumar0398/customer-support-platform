## Contract and changes

- New membership schema/service/router mounted at `/api/memberships`: ADMIN-only scoped roster, existing-account addition by normalized email, and role/active-status updates. No account provisioning, password editing, global directory, or hard deletion.
- Strict body/query/path validation; public DTO contains organizationId/userId/email/role/isActive only. Roster is stably ordered and capped, with count/items in a RepeatableRead transaction.
- All membership writes lock the organization row and then re-read current actor authority. Last active admin protection, duplicate detection, and mutation share the transaction. A request waiting on that lock cannot use a removed admin role.
- Deactivation preserves historical relations. Existing global users may join multiple organizations; adding a known email does not change that account or its other memberships. Known-account existence can be inferred by an authorized admin from the response; invitations/consent onboarding remain outside this MVP workflow.
- README/API documentation updated. No schema/migration/lockfile changes.
